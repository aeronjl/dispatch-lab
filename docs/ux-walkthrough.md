# P2 participant walkthrough

Status: prepared; no participant results recorded by the implementation task.
Allow approximately 15–20 minutes. Use a participant pseudonym and identify the
facilitator. Record the application commit and the recording/project IDs. Keep
participant words separate from the facilitator's interpretation. The existing
Learning & policies → comprehension session facility can preserve the six response
categories and issue records; mark any agent-only rehearsal accordingly.

Start at the quiet simulation. Do not begin by explaining the menu layout. Ask the
participant to think aloud and note their first choice, backtracking and requests
for help. Avoid judging speed when a solver or network request is pending.

1. **Site and design.** “Make a plant project for a European location. Find what we
   know about the land, and change the battery capacity.” Observe whether they
   distinguish a regional anchor from an assessed parcel and the editable design
   from the recording they arrived from.
2. **Operate.** “Prepare a one-day run and explain what its weather represents.”
   Use the synthetic option for a quick rehearsal, chosen explicitly. Visit the
   design and return before running; check whether they expect their choices to
   persist. Note whether calculation and recorded playback are confused.
3. **Investigate.** “Find a period you want to understand and explain one decision.”
   Ask them to identify one assumption, one observation and one prediction. Let
   them choose their own component and evidence route.
4. **Compare.** “Try a different decision using the information available then.”
   Ask what changed and what did not, including the original recording and ending
   inventories. Check whether a prediction is mistaken for realised performance.
5. **Write up.** “Preserve the evidence and your interpretation so someone else can
   read it.” Make another edit after saving. Ask which text an export will contain.
   Also locate the broader study report and explain how it differs from a period
   investigation.
6. **Return and explain limits.** “Return to where you were operating. Explain what
   this evidence establishes and what it cannot establish about a real plant.”
   Check component, controller, interval and keyboard focus rather than merely
   whether the diagram is visible.

For each task record: outcome (unassisted / assisted / blocked), participant's
words or “no answer”, observed behaviour, and any misunderstanding. Classify each
issue as minor or material, with an owner, disposition and the observed retest.
A material confusion about design/run identity, predicted/recorded outcomes,
site weather, publication contents or evidence scope prevents closing the gate.

Do not fill in answers on the participant's behalf or convert automated test
successes into participant results. The current implementation's separate browser
rehearsal lives under `build/ux/` and is described in [the workflow contract](ux-workflow.md).
