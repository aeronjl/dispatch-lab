# Referenced contact inspection

`referenced-contact/1` is an opt-in, bounded sensing mechanism in `plant-service-contracts/5`. It uses the existing fixed reader and rover, separately or together. A prepared electronic test port exposes one module trip contact. An illustrative 24 V excitation represents a closed contact; an open contact gives 0 V. Each acquisition also measures internal zero and 24 V span references. These are model assumptions, not specifications for a particular product. The rover illustration does not imply that an ordinary camera can perform this electrical test or identify hidden damage.

The mobile reader assumes a compatible passive docking probe at that prepared port. Approach and acquisition consume the recorded travel/work time and robot energy. Probe alignment, contact force and electrical protection are not simulated, and no generic manipulation capability is granted to the rover. A real implementation would need a validated interface; the separate prepared-port cost is explicitly unpriced here.

For signal `a`, zero `z` and span `b`, the interpreted voltage is `24 × (a − z) / (b − z)`. The reference is acceptable when the absolute zero reading is at most 2 V and the span is within 10% of 24 V. Within those limits the zero/span correction is applied. A failed reference isolates that reader for the current decision. Corrected values at or below 6 V mean open, and values at or above 18 V mean closed; values between the bands remain uncertain. Inputs, thresholds, units and classifications are recorded. No confidence percentage or causal importance score is invented.

Independent, named bounded-uniform random draws have a configurable standard deviation, initially 0.1 V. Fixed and mobile reader errors use separate streams. Scenario faults can add persistent reader offset, linear drift from a configured onset, or complete dropout. The same additive reader error affects its signal and internal references. Drift accumulates to the fractional acquisition time; fault onset and the physical contact state follow the containing plant interval. A delayed packet becomes available at the first hourly decision boundary after acquisition plus its declared delay. An old packet keeps its original measurement time and cannot become fresh by being read again.

The physical fault port contains those scenario inputs. The service supervisor receives only eligible signal/reference readings, their source identities and declared interpretation thresholds. It does not receive the injected reader fault, true contact state or future restoration time. Changing a later fault cannot change earlier observations or actions. Noise is keyed by reader, acquisition time and channel rather than by unrelated task order.

With paired readers the local rule requests both acquisitions after process tracking establishes a capacity shortfall. It waits for their evidence. Two valid contradictory values remain ambiguous. A valid channel can support the decision when the other reader has failed its references or explicitly returned an unavailable measurement; the isolated channel is named. A contact voltage between the bands with acceptable references is contact uncertainty, not a failed reader, and keeps the combined decision uncertain. Missing or stale evidence does not count as agreement. After the declared investigation wait, unresolved evidence permits a qualified investigative module substitution, with its normal isolation, crew, stock and verification requirements. This is a conservative local rule, not an optimal information-gathering policy or a diagnosis of the underlying damage.

Both readers share the same contact. A contact stuck open or closed can therefore mislead both despite perfect internal reference results. Agreement does not establish module health. A reset changes only a resettable plant trip; it does not repair a contact or reader fault. Post-reset process tracking, not contact agreement or completed work, must establish recovered capacity. Reader calibration/replacement procedures and richer payloads remain part of the wider hardware programme. No sensor fault disappears merely because its inspection is complete.

A completed reset or module-substitution procedure makes contact samples acquired at or before that work historical from the next eligible decision boundary. This applies even when an old packet arrives later, and does not depend on hidden physical success. The report preserves acquisition quality and any failed reader reference separately from current contact eligibility. An old sample cannot establish the contact's post-procedure state; successful operating probes can still establish process recovery without another invasive intervention.

Public records distinguish the acquired evidence from a separate retrospective sample trace containing physical signal, imposed error and random operands. The dependency-free checker reconstructs the signal, reference arithmetic, error stream, publication boundaries, latest applicable evidence and channel selection independently. These checks establish consistency and causality in the declared sandbox. They do not establish field reliability, real sensor independence, electrical protection, hazard certification or empirical fault-detection accuracy.

The fixed reader's standing allocation and each rover mission's actual energy/wear use the existing ledger. A paired read is not a free duplicate observation. Prepared-port and internal-reference installation costs remain part of the stage-3 economics; no full hardware-value conclusion is drawn from these short examples. Inspection tables appear only in the revealed service inspector, preserving the quiet main scene and original equipment artwork.
