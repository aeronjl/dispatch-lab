# Qualified reference autonomy: release-1 contract

The plant remains an hourly scheduling/diagnosis sandbox. Sensors, private
execution outcomes, controller estimates and retrospective truth are distinct.
The independent execution allocator enforces physical limits even when a request
cannot be honoured. The requested and applied actions remain separate.

The version-5 explanatory probe sequence has a numerical resource limit of 1,000
increments. Smaller increments that cannot advance floating-point capacity, or
require a longer sequence, return an explicit unsupported-input error. Inputs are
never silently enlarged and a run is never left in an unbounded calculation.

## Recovery version 5

Choose **Separate recovery deadline and test appointments (version 5)** in advanced
setup. It requires version-3 coordinated services and enabled sensing. Default
package selection uses joint recovery for MPC; Greedy retains local services and
version-1 independent recovery. The UI and recorded policy disclose this package
difference. Explicit common-local policies are used for process-objective studies.
Earlier policy versions are still executable with their original semantics.

A diagnosis opens a finite original obligation. A separate completed whole mission
(including its return and available public receipt) opens its own finite verification
attempt. The original diagnosis deadline and its miss remain recorded. Forecast
updates, interrupted tests and increased capacity estimates cannot extend that
attempt. Another completed remedy creates a separately identified attempt.

The joint process/service/dock planner chooses an individual load-test appointment
within the attempt. The appointment requests one upward capacity increment and
consecutive informative intervals. Successful tracking and unchanged delivery are
explicit planning hypotheses, not diagnoses. Only observed operation updates the
capacity estimate. An appointment is released with an explicit reason if the actual
tracking, work or resources no longer support it.

The record includes the remaining increments and a necessary informative-time
bound. At 218.4 kW estimated capacity, 450 kW nameplate, a 45 kW increment and two
confirmation intervals, six increases are needed: at least twelve fresh intervals,
or eleven with one eligible consecutive observation. Version 4 could expire the
whole recovery at the end of the first four-hour appointment/grace window. Version
5 keeps the configured outer deadline. The necessary-time bound does not guarantee
available sunlight, inventory headroom, successful tracking or feasible later tests.

Inconclusive, resource-blocked tests do not justify repair. Repeated independent
same-load tracking shortfalls support the existing finite follow-up procedure.
Pending remedies are allowed to complete before verification competes for power.
An expired attempt becomes escalation-required and stays unresolved; the supervisor
continues physically feasible degraded operation. A successful procedure receipt
alone never marks it healthy. Disabled sensing never becomes observed confirmation.

## Objectives and reporting boundaries

Physical feasibility has priority over production and economic targets. Methane
MPC and economic MPC remain separate objectives; fixed ownership allocation is not
a dispatch incentive. Report physical production, allocated costs, assumed operating
contribution, human visits, remote hours, service work and solver fallbacks together.
Ending plant/robot inventories and unresolved service obligations are retained.
A short window cannot establish lifetime cost, calibrated reliability or a probability
of unattended operation. Neither learning nor a new hardware family is needed to
exercise this reference contract.

## Inspection and evidence

The Model workspace now groups existing services into seven interactive essays:
cleaning, inspection/references, intervention/recovery, robot charging, finite
logistics, service costs and service uncertainty. Each uses production interfaces;
recovery and charging execute through bounded isolated workers. Current reference
parameters and literature/evidence gaps appear through the existing assumption
registry. Original recorded traces show their own operands and policy identities.
Old recordings without explanations remain explicitly missing; they do not inherit
new explanations as if they had been present originally.

The reference examples are numerically checked, not field-calibrated. The contact
reader shares a physical contact across modalities; agreement cannot exclude common
contact failure. The optical surface monitor remains an idealised assumption. A
human replacement, fixed reset and flow calibration are compatible bounded actions,
not evidence that a mobile robot can perform arbitrary repair. Service durations,
crew logistics, consumables and thermal constants retain their declared uncertainty.
