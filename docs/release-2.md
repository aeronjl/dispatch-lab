# Release 2: deployment and the service lifecycle

Authorised in full on 14 September 2026. Release 1 remains an immutable publication.
The release result, qualification receipts and updated research priorities are in
[the Release-2 report](../research/release-2/report.html). That publication is the
completion record; this document defines the implemented scope and its gates.

## Scope

- Original stage-3 preservation: finish the provision and interface repetitions,
  compare all six original-source editions, retain original outcomes and create
  portable restoration receipts. These repetitions do not qualify new source.
- Fourteen hardware families: executable bounded capabilities or enforced evidence
  restrictions. A catalogue entry never supplies a missing physical mechanism.
- Optional commissioning: dependencies, finite additional project crew, acceptance,
  rework, progressive available capacity, dated expenditure and temporary departure.
- Optional condition: solar calendar loss and PEM operating-exposure hypotheses,
  delayed/noisy observations, finite spares, replacement, measured verification,
  replenishment, support outages and carried unresolved work.
- Maintenance comparisons: none, periodic, observed threshold and forecast-window
  scheduling. Existing process objectives retain their own requested/applied traces.
- Revealed Sites controls and four interactive Model essays, recorded component
  links, chronological accounting, source snapshots, offline reports and new studies.

The [lifecycle contract](lifecycle.md) defines units, clocks, interfaces, accounting
and limitations. [The experiment protocol](../research/release-2/protocol.md) names
matched European environments, policy comparisons and uncertainty challenges.

## Completion gates

1. Preserve original source editions without relabelling old validation as current.
2. Give every family an implemented or evidence-restricted disposition with mechanism,
   prerequisites, observations, costs and evidence scope. No generic invented repair.
3. Reconcile commissioning capacity, labour, energy, acceptance, departure and invoices.
4. Reconcile condition, stock and work across continuous execution and checkpoints;
   observations never receive future outage ends or private procedure outcomes.
5. Publish matched comparisons with terminal energy, feedstock, thermal state,
   condition, stock and unresolved obligations. Retain failed/incomplete cases.
6. Qualify the Sites → design → operation → Model/trace → comparison → write-up path,
   original artwork, legacy/offline behaviour and input-to-display performance.

## Boundaries

New mechanisms are opt-in. Legacy configurations omit the lifecycle block. PV
and PEM rates are scoped literature hypotheses, not equipment calibration. No
remaining-life oracle, certified commissioning, general robotic manipulation or
new biological/chemical mechanism is implied. Current support and target exclusion
are conservative; the forecast-window rule is not globally optimal fleet planning.

The dedicated project crew is additional to existing field service labour. Project
and field procedures cannot silently overlap on a target. A full-array condition
is homogeneous, including partially commissioned capacity; heterogeneous module
populations require a different model. A replacement procedure does not repair an
unrelated injected fault, and successful work is not observed confirmation.

A lifecycle year is not repeated to fabricate a lifetime cash projection. Full
chronological years are required for the requested cash horizon. Ending inventories
have no speculative sale proceeds. Evidence gaps, unsupported capabilities and
uncalibrated distributions remain visible rather than filled with arbitrary numbers.

Release 3 covers reproducible learning, held-out evaluation, deployment interfaces
for bounded policies, participant comprehension and final operational qualification.
