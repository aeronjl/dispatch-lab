   interrupted reproduction where required. Retain prior outcomes and source editions.
2. Give all fourteen families explicit, executable capability restrictions or a
   complete implemented mechanism, with prerequisites, costs, evidence and a

4. Integrate condition, maintenance, replacement, support outages and replenishment

5. Compare no maintenance, fixed schedules, condition-based work and planning under

6. Expose setup and results through Sites, Model and existing analysis, preserving the
   quiet operation screen and original artwork. Publish a continuous site/deployment/
   maintenance example, source-bound checks, offline outputs and revised roadmap.

## Design boundaries

New mechanisms are opt-in and versioned. Old configurations remain byte-equivalent
when these options are absent. Empirical parameter support, declared scenario ranges
and independent numerical checks remain separate. Construction rates and remedy
compatibility must not be inferred from a generic robot label. Unsupported automatic
manipulation, metrology, wall inspection and sample-analysis capabilities remain
explicitly unavailable until their prerequisite models and evidence exist.

Commissioning is a dependency/work/acceptance model, not a pressure-system or
electrical safety certificate. Calendar PV decline and usage-related stack voltage
growth are reduced hypotheses, with explicit parameters, units, sensitivity ranges
and source applicability. No universal remaining-life oracle is introduced.
