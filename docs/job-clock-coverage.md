# Variable work time preserves treatment area

A cleaning job's private physical duration includes both the persistent equipment
factor and its accepted-job multiplier. Its surface progress uses the section area
divided by that actual perform-stage duration. It does not reuse a nominal speed
or only the persistent factor. A completed pass traverses one section; an
interruption at a declared fraction retains that fraction of area. Brush and
water supplies scale by area, while labour and energy scale by time.

The broader 13 September recovery qualification exposed this missing coupling in
an optical cleaning case (fixed mode, event seed 29). The original independently
failed trace and its source remain in programme
`e33a469eb8264c89922d199ba7f2292c`. Subsequent source editions correct execution;
they do not relabel that trace as valid. Its brush-efficacy discrepancies were
about 0.00014–0.00055 against an approximately 0.00001 tolerance. No tolerance was
relaxed. A new direct ledger-versus-coverage invariant strengthens both runtime
and independent checks, rather than relying only on the indirect efficacy check.

This correction changes neither hardware capability nor the assumed removal
fraction. Tests independently require a full section's area and brush use for a
completed pass, and 30% for the declared interruption, with variable job clocks.
Portable treatment retains its separate water and adhered-fouling assumptions.
