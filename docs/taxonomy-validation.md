## Practical boundaries

This catalogue does not calibrate plant or robot behaviour, grant repair permissions, or remove the realism review's evidence gaps. A family classification and a matching action are not proof that a physical repair is feasible. Objects without a dedicated observed operation display say so; initial resource contracts are not presented as remaining stock. Older archives remain unchanged and do not acquire original catalogue evidence retrospectively.

Supporting artifacts are under `build/taxonomy/`: desktop/mobile screenshots, the service fixture and reproduction bundles, `offline-check.json`, `performance.json`, and the unpacked offline catalogue and player. Older generated editions remain preserved.
