# Conditional investigation and repair

This is an experimental calculation interface within stage 4 of the six-stage field programme. It does not replace the live service controller. `contact-investigation-belief/1`, `conditional-service-work/1`, `inspection-contingency-planning/1` and `contact-investigation-learning/1` connect current evidence to possible inspection findings, compatible future work, process scheduling and explicit ending obligations.

## Current information and assumptions

The comparison starts with the observed capacity estimate, plant inventory, eligible forecast, accepted service commitments, original accumulated cost operands and installed capability catalogue. It has no simulator effect port, injected fault schedule, scheduled recovery time or private outcome draw. Teaching packets are independently declared examples; they are not reconstructed historical evidence.

The initial illustrative hypothesis weights are 0.5 resettable trip, 0.3 equipment damage and 0.2 damage with a stuck closed contact. Both latter hypotheses need compatible replacement; a reset cannot repair physical damage. Each hypothesis can also declare static fixed/mobile reader offset or dropout. The reader likelihood uses the execution reference/band classifier with finite midpoint quadrature over its independent bounded noise channels. The quadrature resolution, offsets and prior source are recorded. These probabilities are assumptions, not calibrated reliability estimates. All branches use the original derated capacity estimate as their conditional pre-intervention delivery; this prototype does not model uncertainty in that estimate's magnitude or a healthy-module false-alarm hypothesis.

Conditioning uses `P(h | finding) = P(finding | h) P(h) / Σ P(finding | h′) P(h′)`. A noiseless closed contact gives posterior weights 5/7 trip and 2/7 stuck contact under the initial fixture. Agreement from another reader does not remove the common contact hypothesis. Raw voltages, zero/span references, timing and original source identifiers remain attached to each observation. Duplicate copies do not add evidence. Missing, stale or pre-intervention packets do not become healthy readings. An observation outside the declared support returns an unsupported result, with no arbitrary probability floor.

A prior is bound to a static-condition epoch. A later observed intervention cutoff invalidates an earlier prior for automatic reuse. The caller must declare a new current-epoch prior or supply a separately validated transition model. Discarding old readings does not justify silently resetting the original probabilities. This prototype does not yet implement that dynamic transition model.

## Conditional work and production

The two arms are immediate qualified module replacement and inspection followed by reset for a usable closed contact or qualified replacement otherwise. A future guard is explicitly a prediction, never a fabricated `Reading`. The original capability still declares its unmet contact requirement. Conditional adapters are rejected by actual mission submission; execution must rebuild a compatible recipe when the finding arrives.

Crew response lead starts when a conditional request becomes known. Whole work/return, current stock, tool power peaks, shared occupancy, isolation and finite crew time are checked. Two separate crew departures cannot be promised without observing a return; compatible shared itineraries remain the existing visit interface. Accepted work is retained. Optical arrays require their original reference radiation and current surface/treatment evidence, including any ongoing cleaning commitment. There is no constant-soiling substitution.

Mobile contact-reading failure occurs at perform completion before sampling, matching the execution adapter. The failed branch spends outbound/work energy and entered materials but does not perform verification or return. Launch feasibility still reserves the complete original return route and margin. A stranded rover, missing observation and outstanding retrieval/readiness work remain explicit. An unreadable completed sensor sample is a different outcome and uses its own likelihood.

Each physically compatible action branches on the declared qualified-procedure reliability. Incompatible actions get no restoration benefit. Process requests share the same schedule until the observation history distinguishes branches. Latent repair success changes predicted delivered capacity, not the requested controls or the controller's capacity estimate. Two consecutive nameplate requests are planned after intervention and return, then the request limit returns to the original estimate. Actual tracking is still required for confirmation; every branch ends explicitly unverified in this prediction.

Scenario solver version 3 adds observation-dependent request bounds while preserving the version 2 interface for callers that do not use them. Bounds cannot differ before their observation or contradict shared bounds. Solver termination, feasible incumbents and unresolved solves remain visible. No global service-routing optimum is claimed.

## Costs, missing continuations and preservation

Conditional service cost is the accumulated prefix-plus-continuation decision pool less already incurred cost. Fixed ownership and speculative terminal sale proceeds are excluded. Electricity and production losses enter their physical calculations and are not charged again as duplicate penalties. Ending service stocks reflect projected consumption; unselected queued work and stranded recovery obligations remain visible. A failed intervention still consumes its applicable labour, parts and usage.

Every positive-probability finding needs a feasible continuation. If one is unavailable—for example, a crew arrives beyond the horizon—the arm is incomplete. Feasible branches retain their original probability mass; they are not renormalized and no partial aggregate is presented as complete. Existing nonconstant capacity/probe commitments are currently refused explicitly until the joint preservation interface is connected.

Run `.venv/bin/python -m methane.services.investigation_examples` to save six independent cases: fixed reader, mobile interruption, common-contact sensitivity, reader dropout, late crew and insufficient power. Each directory retains sealed public input and result packets; the suite stores an immutable source capsule and human-readable offline report. Recalculate an input with `--replay PATH --directory DESTINATION`. Changed source is labelled, and publication preserves different earlier outputs. Finite solver reruns can differ; an unchanged playback record and a new numerical result are distinct artifacts.
