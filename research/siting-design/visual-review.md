# Report visual review — 13 September 2026

Inspected the browser-generated desktop overview, resource chart and narrow-screen
resource view recorded in `browser-validation.json`. The amber/charcoal palette
and local Departure Mono render correctly; the desktop index remains separate
from the readable essay, and the narrow-screen controls and exact numerical
readout remain accessible without horizontal page overflow. The chart uses a
common scale across all three sites. Small chart labels on mobile are supplemented
by the month selector and full textual values.

This is a visual review of the research artifact. It does not establish usability
of the proposed map workspace, change application screenshot baselines, or stand
in for a user comprehension walkthrough.
