# Release 2: product acceptance revision

User-directed revision, 14 September 2026. This supersedes the requirement to finish
all 116 research cases and all six large historical exports before release. It does
not rewrite the original protocol, programme identities or incomplete executions.

## Named acceptance cases

| Check | Purpose | Evidence scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lifecycle reference and independent checks | Capacity, condition, stock, costs, observation boundary and continuity | Source-bound R2 qualification; synthetic mechanisms, not field calibration |
| Six-hour Sites browser workflow | Configure, execute, original-interval playback, compare complete/incomplete cases, edit report, export, reuse template | Product integration, not a policy comparison |
| Template/report regression | Correct seed/configuration reuse; revisions preserve frozen physical values; incomplete publications stay incomplete | New workflow source |
| Cancel/resume checkpoint fixtures | Keep successful hours and original source; failed interval cannot mutate saved state | Automated tests plus preserved case-038 cancellation/resumption |
| One 8,760-hour London case | Carry clocks, stocks and state over a year and inspect long-duration performance | One declared fixture; does not establish annual policy superiority |
| 72-hour lifecycle offline bundle | Restore original explanations and calculations without network access | Captured-source offline check, not a new scientific conclusion |
| Reviewed interface and bounded latency | Preserve plant/solar art, usable revealed controls and responsive interaction | Controlled fixture; the separate archive-contention miss remains disclosed |

Additional cases need a named mechanism, boundary, defect or workflow justification.
No acceptance check requires MPC or a maintenance policy to win. Model changes
trigger relevant regression checks; published scientific results retain their own
source identities and are not silently relabelled.

## Optional research programmes

The seasonal, annual-policy, disclosed-uncertainty and archived-forecast comparisons
remain frozen recipes. Completed seasonal work is available for inspection; the
annual study is intentionally partial after its first representative case. The
unexecuted cases make no research claim. Users can open these templates, change
saved plant designs, retain or change weather inputs, then choose to execute them.

All 702 earlier numerical repetitions already exist. Four large portable exports
were fully restored; the remaining two formatting jobs were stopped at the scope
revision. Their partial artifacts and original completed numerical records remain
on disk. Finishing every redundant export is not necessary to verify the export
feature. No ignored evidence is deleted to declare completion.

## Source boundaries

The annual case and previous numerical qualification use their captured source.
The subsequent workflow changes add recipes, authored report editions and elapsed
partition timing; they do not change the physics or dispatch model. New focused
tests qualify those changes under their new identity. Prior qualification is never
presented as having run against the later source.
