The remaining completion roadmap

Release 1 establishes the reference system and its research evidence. Release 2 completes deployment and maintenance mechanisms. Release 3 adds reproducible learning and finishes the integrated research product. The original six-stage field programme remains a historical record; completed studies must be reused, and its paused queue must not be silently restarted.

Release 2 · deployment and the service lifecycle

  1. Close preservation and comparison obligations. Inventory the original stage-3 sensitivity bundles and their source reruns; complete the outstanding checks without rerunning already published tiers. Carry unresolved work, inventories, uncertain capacity and support condition through extended comparison windows. Keep the Release-1 numerical variation visible when selecting baselines.
  2. Give all fourteen hardware families an explicit implementation disposition. Extend and compare the existing row cleaner, portable cleaner, ground inspector, fixed sensing and bounded actuator/service mechanisms. Add aerial inspection, contact inspection, vegetation management, sampling, automatic sensor service, manipulation, prepared module interfaces, construction and deployable arrays only with their requisite physical or information mechanisms. A family may be represented as evidence-restricted rather than granted an unsupported capability.
  3. Complete shared infrastructure and commissioning. Model access, docks, tools, references, communications, consumables, crew and remote assistance as dependencies that can fail. Add mobilisation, installation progress, inspection/acceptance and progressive commissioned capacity. Temporary construction equipment can leave; costs and capacity dates flow into Sites.
  4. Carry condition across the lifecycle. Add supported degradation hypotheses, replacements, replenishment and backlogs. Separate operating exposure from calendar ageing, and a recycled weather year from an independent weather sample. Treat calibration and equipment quotations as evidence acquisition, with explicit gaps.
  5. Compare maintenance policies. Evaluate fixed schedules, condition-based intervention and service-aware planning with isolation, production, charging, crew visits and failed remedies. Predict a remaining useful life only when the degradation mechanism and observation chain support that claim.
  6. Publish site/design/lifecycle comparisons. Extend Sites into a continuous deployment-to-operation example with clear screening versus detailed simulation boundaries. Each family needs a mechanism, bounded compatibility, resources, costs, observations, tests, illustration, Model explanation and comparative question before it is called numerically implemented.

Release-2 gate: the full agreed taxonomy has implemented or evidence-restricted dispositions; a continuous deployment-to-maintenance example reconciles plant availability, condition, resources, expenditure and unresolved obligations. Sensitivity results retain conclusion reversals and incomplete exposure.

Release 3 · learning and the finished research workflow

  1. Provide reproducible training machinery. Build observation-only environments, recorded datasets, frozen train/evaluation partitions, model registration, training manifests and policy deployment interfaces. Keep training outside playback and preserve data, seed, runtime and model identities.
  2. Evaluate learning in increasing scope. Begin with estimators and planning aids; then test scheduling and homeostatic/reserve approaches against the Release-1 references. Retain physical execution limits, bounded service capabilities, fallback and explicit production/economic objectives.
  3. Run held-out qualification. Separate training from unseen weather, equipment and plant configurations. Distinguish solver repeats, event seeds and independent environmental samples. Publish failures, uncertainty/support violations, data and computation costs, and cases where learning brings no benefit.
  4. Finish the end-to-end product. Complete Sites → design → operation → investigation → comparison → write-up, including service/lifecycle/learning essays, result lineage, accessible interaction and quiet illustrated operation.
  5. Qualify delivery and comprehension. Verify fresh installation, interruption/resumption, offline restore, old archives, runtime/performance budgets and durable artifact handling. Conduct actual participant walkthroughs; the current agent-led review does not substitute for them.

Release-3 gate: a fresh installation can execute the guided demonstration, restore a published study offline and create a new numerical edition with reported differences. The capabilities and evidence limits must be understandable, testable and traceable. No requirement that every robot is useful or every learned policy wins.

Evidence work alongside both releases

Prioritise observations that could change the architecture: cause/remedy compatibility, independent sensor/reference quality, reactor heat retention, soiling and array conversion, degradation exposure, access/isolation/return conditions, crew and material logistics, and dated commercial inputs. The evidence acquisition plan specifies the next measurements. These require equipment/site evidence; software cannot manufacture calibration from repeatability.

Field validation, live control, certification, contractual payments and DePIN implementation remain separate from these three software releases. The next implementation package belongs to Release 2; this release has not started that work.
