Workflow verification with illustrative assumptions, ideal current-weather and surface monitors, a short horizon and bounded solver budgets. Not field calibration or a hardware ranking.

What these runs establish

All 18 world/seed cases completed across the three knowledge arms, producing 54 controller traces. In the two mismatch worlds, mean one-step available-power error across controllers and seeds fell from 45.88 kW with fixed assumptions to 23.24 kW with adaptation. This is a conditional result in these authored scenarios, not an external calibration result.

The solar estimate approached 0.758 of nominal conversion in the mismatch cases. Greedy performed enough cleaning to bring its expected removal close to the injected 0.2 value. Methane MPC learned less when it performed fewer passes. Economic MPC performed no cleaning in these short windows and retained its initial removal assumption: there was no informative service evidence.

Greedy production was unchanged by adaptation. Methane MPC produced more methane on average in the two mismatch worlds, while economic MPC results were mixed. These numbers do not isolate a policy benefit: even the nominal worlds, with identical physical and controller assumptions, showed MPC production differences across arms under the short wall-clock solver budgets. Benchmark and verification work ran concurrently. A performance claim requires controlled, repeated computation trials with sufficient budgets; solver termination and fallback counts are retained here.

The completed studies preserve their original source capsules. The separate current-release demonstration additionally checks legacy-service compatibility and clarified residual labels. Earlier recorded calculations have not been rewritten as current-source evidence.

Interpretation boundaries

The solar multiplier describes effective performance under assumed observed irradiance; it does not identify the physical cause. Cleaning estimates require informative work and the ideal surface monitor. A controller that defers work may collect no cleaning evidence. One-step power error includes changing weather and services, not only conversion mismatch. Different actions can expose different service events despite shared random streams.

Estimation accuracy does not imply greater methane production. Limited solves, different objectives, short comparison windows and ending inventories can change rankings. The linked write-ups retain costs, downtime, all ending inventories, incomplete cases and original inputs. Repeat this script to create new editions after changing the plant; retain the earlier editions for comparison.
