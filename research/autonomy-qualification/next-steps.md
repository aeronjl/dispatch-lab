# After this qualification

This programme does not resume the paused expansion to fourteen hardware families.
It preserves the tested policies, including failures, and does not promote a new
controller on the basis of production totals alone.

1. **Correct and qualify the recovery deadline contract.** Version 4 currently
   turns a window for one upward load test into a deadline for complete recovery.
   Give individual test appointments and the overall recovery obligation separate
   identities and outcomes. Preserve an immutable overall bound; count the
   remaining confirmation steps; replan missed appointments from the observations
   and forecasts available then. Successful first tracking must not itself end the
   incident, while exhaustion of the overall bound must remain explicit. Add
   independent deadline/count checks and causal tests, then run new matched policy
   editions on the declared capacity, bias, failed-remedy and null cases. Preserve
   outage, lost power and insufficient-evidence cases. Do not quietly extend old
   deadlines or use injected recovery truth to schedule tests.
2. **Resolve close comparisons at adequate numerical budgets.** The long-horizon,
   0.1-second repeats are visibly unstable. Use larger budgets and multiple
   numerical repeats for affected rankings; report incumbent/gap/fallback and
   original traces. Compare process objectives under common service policies,
   then compare complete packages as a separately named question. Neither equal
   methane totals nor identical seeds establish identical decisions.
3. **Acquire evidence before widening capability claims.** Use the parameter-bound
   evidence plan for cause/remedy compatibility, observed post-service tests,
   sensor calibration, crew response, spares, delivered CO₂, thermal transients, DC generation
   and quotations. Fit on distinct observation periods and validate on held-out
   periods. Continue to show unsupported capabilities as unavailable. Conditional
   maintenance exposure is not an annual failure-rate model. Several fixture results reach the CO₂ supply ceiling; compare explicit supply scenarios before inferring the value of additional power or storage.
4. **Extend environmental qualification after the controller gate.** The current
   seasonal cases are seven-day windows in an already-used 2025 year and use
   persistence forecasts. Add separately held-out years, actual forecast vintages,
   adverse access conditions and wider credible equipment ranges once the required
   inputs are available. Rank sites conditionally and retain a no-build option;
   regional coordinates are not surveyed, permitted parcels.
5. **Only then consider a learned policy or another hardware family.** Establish
   the mechanism it should improve, a credible capability envelope, matched
   baseline, training/evaluation separation, safety constraints and cost ledger
   first. Preserve the readable fixed-policy reference and recovery fallback.

The preview speed change is independent of these numerical policy changes. Its
measurements must distinguish the first edit, repeated edits, rendering and
contention on the stated fixture. A pass for a local fixture is not a guarantee
for a remote client, arbitrary archive size or unbounded batch concurrency.
